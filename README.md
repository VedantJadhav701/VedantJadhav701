## 🧠 About Me

I'm an **AI Systems Engineer and Machine Learning Researcher** focused on transitioning intelligent systems from theoretical research prototypes into optimized, production-grade deployments.

Currently pursuing my **B.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at Pimpri Chinchwad University, Pune** (2023–27).

- **Efficient Deep Learning** (Mamba/SSMs, KV-Cache Quantization, Knowledge Distillation)
- **Domain-Specific Language Models** (Medical, Educational, Financial & Telecom Reasoning)
- **Agentic Architectures** (Autonomous Workflows, Planner-Specialist Coordination, Multi-Agent Systems)
- **MLOps & Infrastructure** (Dockerized inference pipelines, statistical drift monitoring, sub-10ms serving)
